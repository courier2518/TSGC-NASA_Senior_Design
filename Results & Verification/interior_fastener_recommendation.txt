
INTERIOR STAINLESS STEEL FASTENER RECOMMENDATION
===============================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
----------------
Stainless steel fasteners are IDEAL for interior habitat applications.
No significant galvanic corrosion concerns in controlled atmosphere.
No special isolation or coatings required.

KEY FINDINGS:
✓ Corrosion risk: NEGLIGIBLE (< 0.5 μm/year at interface)
✓ Thermal expansion: NEGLIGIBLE (9°C range, 0.001mm movement)
✓ Strength: MORE than adequate (typical MS > 5.0)
✓ Cost: OPTIMAL (standard COTS hardware)
✓ Installation: SIMPLE (no special procedures)

==============================================================
3. STANDARD INTERIOR FASTENER SPECIFICATION
==============================================================

PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION:
-----------------------
Material: 316 Stainless Steel (A4-70 grade)
Finish: Passivated (ASTM A967) or as-received
Head style: Socket cap or button head (no sharp edges)

SIZE SELECTION:
• M4: Light fixtures, panels, small electronics (< 10 kg)
• M5: Displays, control panels, light equipment (10-30 kg)
• M6: Storage racks, workstations, standard equipment (30-60 kg)
• M8: Exercise equipment, heavy ECLSS units (> 60 kg)

INSTALLATION:
• NO isolation washers required
• NO sealants required
• NO special coatings required
• Standard torque values apply
• Use split lock washers for vibrating equipment
• Thread locker (Loctite 242) optional for critical items

==============================================================
4. ADVANTAGES FOR INTERIOR USE
==============================================================

VERSUS EXTERIOR/STRUCTURAL CONNECTIONS:
• No galvanic isolation needed (saves time/mass)
• No temperature cycling (-173°C to +127°C → just 9°C range)
• No vacuum/pressure differential
• No radiation exposure
• No micrometeorite risk

VERSUS TITANIUM OR EXOTIC ALLOYS:
• 90% cost reduction
• Immediate availability
• Standard tools and procedures
• Identical performance in benign environment
• Better machinability for custom brackets

==============================================================
5. SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS
==============================================================

VIBRATING EQUIPMENT (ECLSS, Exercise):
• Use lock washers or Nordlock washers
• Apply medium-strength thread locker
• Inspect annually for loosening
• Consider safety wire for critical connections

CREW INTERFACES:
• Deburr all threads and edges
• Use button head screws where possible
• Install protective caps on exposed threads
• Ensure 6mm minimum edge distance

MAINTENANCE:
• Visual inspection annually
• Re-torque after first 30 days (settling)
• Replace if any visible corrosion (unlikely)
• Keep spare fasteners in common sizes

==============================================================
